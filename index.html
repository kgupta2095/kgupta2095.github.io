How I work

Prototype

Working prototypes, not vague tickets

I build the first version with AI tools to pressure-test scope, then hand engineering a spec-driven brief they can estimate honestly.

Ship

PRs for the small stuff

I write code and raise pull requests for BAU fixes, so the engineering team stays on the problems that need them.

Automate

Agentic workflows with a stop

I run agents across tasks and comms to move requests toward production, with a human gate before anything becomes a commitment.

Measure

Evals before release

Guardrails, fallbacks, and eval suites are defined in the spec. A feature that cannot pass its own audit does not ship.

An LLM support-ticket summariser where the guardrail is the product: every claim is verified against the source, and one unsupported claim blocks the summary entirely.

Worth probing: the guardrail has its own eval suite, six trap cases with planted hallucinations, because an unmeasured safety check is a decorative one.

Adoption and retention analysis for a care-workforce SaaS, on a reproducible dataset: who actually adopts an AI feature, and whether adoption changes who comes back.
